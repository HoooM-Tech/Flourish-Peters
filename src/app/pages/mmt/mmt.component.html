About Much More Tribe (MMT)

The Much More Tribe (MMT) is an exclusive mentorship programme designed to help you unlock your God-given potential and achieve lasting transformation in all aspects of life. Led by Dr. Flourish Peters, a seasoned mentor with over 20 years of experience in ministry and counseling, MMT is structured to equip you with actionable strategies for a balanced, Impactful life.

By focusing on five critical areas - Faith, Family, Dynamics, Finance, Purpose and Personal Branding. You’ll gain clarity and thrive through principles that align with God’s Word.

This is not just another mentorship programme, It’s a movement. Step into your purpose, live a life of influence and join a tribe committed to rising above mediocrity.
